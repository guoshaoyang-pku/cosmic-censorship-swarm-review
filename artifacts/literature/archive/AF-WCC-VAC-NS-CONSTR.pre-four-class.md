# AF-WCC-VAC-NS-CONSTR

*Explicit vacuum naked-singularity constructions (non-generic)*

Working status: 1 accepted, 1 provisional/unresolved.

## T-208 — Rodnianski-Shlapentokh-Rothman 2023: vacuum naked-singularity exterior solution [accepted]

**Evidence level (derived).** `peer-reviewed`

**Exact statement.** There exist solutions of the 3+1-dimensional Einstein vacuum equations corresponding to the exterior region of a naked singularity, constructed via a new type of self-similarity and a geometric twisting phenomenon.

**Assumptions.** Einstein vacuum equations in 3+1 dimensions; Self-similar (twisted) ansatz; The construction gives the exterior region; the full maximal development requires gluing to an interior solution

**Conclusion type.** `counterexample`

**Sources.** SRC-002, SRC-003, SRC-068

**Scope caveats.** Published Ann. of Math. 198(1) 231-391 (2023); now confirmed by a direct Crossref record (SRC-068), closing the earlier metadata gap.; Exterior solution alone is not a complete counterexample to WCC: one must glue to the interior (SRC-003, preprint) and verify the resulting maximal development.; The abstract itself says the only previous naked singularities were Christodoulou's scalar-field ones (plus numerical/higher-dimensional), and this work 'initiates' the vacuum study.

**Does not imply.** Does not refute generic WCC (the data are special/self-similar).; Does not establish that the glued object is the MGHD of smooth AF data until SRC-003 is verified.

**Unresolved.** Journal reference for SRC-002 not machine-verified on the primary page (L1 gap).; SRC-003 (interior) is an unpublished 2022 preprint; gluing theorem not peer-reviewed.; Whether the exterior data arise from an open set of AF initial data is not claimed.

**Falsifiers.**

- Find an error in the self-similar construction.
- Show the exterior cannot be glued to any interior solution satisfying the vacuum equations, which would invalidate the naked-singularity interpretation.
- Show the resulting spacetime is not the maximal development of regular AF data.

## T-209 — Shlapentokh-Rothman 2022: vacuum naked-singularity interior solution and gluing [provisional]

**Evidence level (derived).** `preprint`

**Exact statement.** There exist solutions of the 3+1-dimensional Einstein vacuum equations corresponding to the interior region of a naked singularity, and the interior and exterior solutions can be glued to produce a naked singularity.

**Assumptions.** Einstein vacuum equations in 3+1 dimensions; Self-similar construction from the exterior paper; Gluing of interior and exterior across a null boundary

**Conclusion type.** `counterexample`

**Sources.** SRC-003

**Scope caveats.** Preprint v1 (April 2022, 166 pages); no journal reference located.; Everything about genericity and about being the MGHD of AF data depends on this unrefereed gluing.

**Does not imply.** Does not refute generic WCC.; Does not imply SCC-related statements about Cauchy horizons.

**Unresolved.** Refereeing status (re-check at next checkpoint).; Whether the glued spacetime is the maximal development of regular asymptotically flat initial data.

**Falsifiers.**

- Find a gap in the gluing construction.
- Show the resulting maximal development is extendible or not AF, changing the counterexample status.
