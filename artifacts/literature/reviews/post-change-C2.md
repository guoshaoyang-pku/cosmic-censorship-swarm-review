A secondary restatement (Liu–Li, arXiv:1710.02922, CMP 363(2):561-578) does state Christodoulou's theorem for BV data with codimension ≥ 1 (≥ 2), so the field is materially plausible — but as written it is unsupported by T-101's cited evidence and inconsistent with its own fix note. **Action: cite the supporting restatement or drop the BV parenthetical.**
