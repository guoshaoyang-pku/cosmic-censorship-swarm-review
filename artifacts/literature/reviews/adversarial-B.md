## 2. Attribution audit: C^0 formulation — Penrose or Christodoulou? (item 2)

Verified attributions in the cited primary sources:

- **Penrose:** SRC-004 only — Dafermos & Luk, Ann. of Math. 202(2) (2025) / arXiv:1710.01722 (fetched 2026-09-11): "the C^0-inextendibility formulation of **Penrose's** celebrated strong cosmic censorship conjecture is in fact false". Note the possessive is ambiguous: it does not by itself say Penrose formulated C^0-inextendibility.
- **Christodoulou:** three independent Dafermos-side primary sources: SRC-021 (Dafermos 2005, CPAM, Wiley abstract: "under **Christodoulou's C^0 formulation**, the strong cosmic censorship conjecture is false for this system"); SRC-056 (same paper, arXiv:gr-qc/0307013, independently fetched: identical wording); SRC-061 (Dafermos & Rodnianski 2005, Invent. Math. 162, 381-457, independently fetched: "Under **Christodoulou's C^0 formulation**, the conjecture is proven to be false").
- **No attribution:** SRC-057/SRC-058 (Luk & Oh) say "the C^0-formulation of the strong cosmic censorship conjecture" without naming anyone.

Verdict on D-002's handling: **partial**. The scope_caveat accurately reports the wording conflict, but the unresolved item "Which source first stated the C^0 formulation precisely (Penrose 1974 lecture vs Christodoulou)" is not supported by any of the four quotes: Penrose 1974 ("Gravitational collapse", IAU Symp. 64, 82-91) appears only in the bibliography of SRC-033 (INSPIRE record 1674350), never in a C^0-formulation attribution. On the quoted evidence, the naming split is 3-1 in favour of "Christodoulou's C^0 formulation", and the sole "Penrose" occurrence is a possessive phrase in a 2025 paper by the same author group that earlier wrote "Christodoulou's". Recommended: record the four exact quotes, reclassify "Penrose first stated C^0" as unproven, and fix the inverted C^0-extensions sentence (section 1, D-002).

4. **T-511 / SRC-066 — wrong journal page range.** "J. Funct. Anal. 271 (2016) 2189-2235" vs Crossref DOI 10.1016/j.jfa.2016.06.013 = pages **1948-1995**; SRC-066 also lacks the DOI.
5. **D-006 — one of four sources does not support the claim.** SRC-014's recorded evidence is the 1999 abstract only and contains no statement about perturbation regularity/functional framework.
6. **Systemic class-tag contamination (S-1).** Eight in-scope entries carry AF-SCC-*-VAC-GEN class_ids for matter-model or test-field results (T-304, T-306, T-501, T-505, T-506, T-510, T-511, T-514); if class tags drive dossier population, the vacuum dossiers will be populated by non-vacuum theorems.
