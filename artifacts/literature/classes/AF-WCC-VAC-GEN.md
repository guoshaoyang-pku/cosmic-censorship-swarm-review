## D-001 — Weak cosmic censorship (WCC), statement of record [verified]

**Evidence level (derived).** `peer-reviewed` · **L0 verification.** `abstract-read`

**Exact statement.** Generically, all singularities in General Relativity arising from regular asymptotically flat initial data should have a complete future null infinity.

**Assumptions.** Einstein field equations (stated for asymptotically flat initial data); A genericity notion must be supplied separately (topology on the data space); the conjecture as quoted does not fix it; Regular initial data

**Conclusion type.** `open_problem`

**Sources.** SRC-001, SRC-011

**Scope caveats.** This is an open conjecture, not a theorem.; SRC-011 (Penrose 1969) is metadata-verified only; the wording quoted is from the peer-reviewed 2025 review SRC-001.

**Does not imply.** Nothing about strong cosmic censorship.; No statement about non-asymptotically-flat (AdS, compact, or cosmological) settings.

**Unresolved.** Exact original wording in Penrose 1969 not machine-retrieved (paywalled, no arXiv).; No canonical genericity topology agreed in the literature.

**Falsifiers.**

- Construct a generic family (open set, or positive measure in an explicitly fixed topology) of asymptotically flat initial data satisfying the constraints whose maximal globally hyperbolic development has incomplete future null infinity.
- Prove that no such genericity notion can make the conjecture true while keeping the known non-generic naked-singularity examples excluded.

## T-201 — Christodoulou 2009: trapped-surface formation from short-pulse vacuum data [verified]

**Evidence level (derived).** `peer-reviewed` · **L0 verification.** `abstract-read`

**Exact statement.** For the Einstein vacuum equations in 3+1 dimensions with no symmetry assumptions, there is an open set of regular initial conditions on an outgoing null hypersurface, of short-pulse type, whose future development forms a trapped surface; the theorems are the first long-time dynamics result for data not confined to a small neighbourhood of Minkowski data.

**Assumptions.** Vacuum Einstein equations; No symmetry assumed; Characteristic initial data on an outgoing null hypersurface plus incoming Minkowski data; Short-pulse (large-amplitude, small-width) incoming gravitational waves

**Conclusion type.** `theorem`

**Sources.** SRC-041, SRC-042, SRC-054

**Scope caveats.** SRC-041 is the MG12 conference chapter (2012, pp. 24-34); the EMS Monograph is SRC-054 (arXiv:0805.3880) with DOI 10.4171/068 verified via Crossref.; Short-pulse data are special, not generic in the space of all AF data.; Trapped-surface formation is not by itself complete future null infinity (WCC), though it is the standard precursor.

**Does not imply.** Does not prove WCC for generic AF vacuum data.; Does not construct a black-hole exterior with complete I+.

**Unresolved.** Precise statement and constants of the main theorem not extracted from the book.; Relation between 'trapped surface forms' and 'I+ is complete' needs the standard global argument, not contained in this theorem.

**Falsifiers.**

- Find an error in the short-pulse mechanism (no retraction found in the verified set).
- Show the trapped surface forms but the solution still has incomplete I+ (would decouple the two notions and change the WCC evidence map).

## T-204 — DHRT 2021: nonlinear stability of Schwarzschild with complete future null infinity [verified]

**Evidence level (derived).** `preprint` · **L0 verification.** `abstract-read`

**Exact statement.** General vacuum initial data with no symmetry, sufficiently close to Schwarzschild data and constrained to a teleologically constructed codimension-3 submanifold of moduli space, evolve to a vacuum spacetime that (i) possesses a complete future null infinity I+ whose past is bounded by a regular future-complete event horizon, (ii) remains close to Schwarzschild in the exterior, and (iii) asymptotes back to a member of the Schwarzschild family.

**Assumptions.** Einstein vacuum equations, no symmetry; Initial data sufficiently close to Schwarzschild; Data constrained to a codimension-3 submanifold of moduli space (teleologically constructed)

**Conclusion type.** `stability_result`

**Sources.** SRC-037, SRC-036

**Scope caveats.** Preprint (513 pages, 2021); no journal reference on arXiv as of 2026-09-11.; Clause (i) is a rigorous positive WCC-type statement only for near-Schwarzschild data.; The codimension-3 restriction means the result does not cover generic perturbations.

**Does not imply.** Does not prove WCC for generic AF vacuum data.; Does not say anything about the black-hole interior beyond the event horizon (no SCC content).

**Unresolved.** Peer-review status.; Whether the codimension-3 restriction can be removed or replaced by the Kerr family.

**Falsifiers.**

- Find an error in the 513-page preprint (no retraction found).
- Show the submanifold restriction is empty, making the theorem vacuous.

## T-208 — Rodnianski-Shlapentokh-Rothman 2023: vacuum naked-singularity exterior solution [verified]

**Evidence level (derived).** `peer-reviewed` · **L0 verification.** `abstract-read`

**Exact statement.** There exist solutions of the 3+1-dimensional Einstein vacuum equations corresponding to the exterior region of a naked singularity, constructed via a new type of self-similarity and a geometric twisting phenomenon.

**Assumptions.** Einstein vacuum equations in 3+1 dimensions; Self-similar (twisted) ansatz; The construction gives the exterior region; the full maximal development requires gluing to an interior solution

**Conclusion type.** `counterexample`

**Sources.** SRC-002, SRC-003, SRC-068

**Scope caveats.** Published Ann. of Math. 198(1) 231-391 (2023); now confirmed by a direct Crossref record (SRC-068), closing the earlier metadata gap.; Exterior solution alone is not a complete counterexample to WCC: one must glue to the interior (SRC-003, preprint) and verify the resulting maximal development.; The abstract itself says the only previous naked singularities were Christodoulou's scalar-field ones (plus numerical/higher-dimensional), and this work 'initiates' the vacuum study.

**Does not imply.** Does not refute generic WCC (the data are special/self-similar).; Does not establish that the glued object is the MGHD of smooth AF data until SRC-003 is verified.

**Unresolved.** Journal reference for SRC-002 not machine-verified on the primary page (L1 gap).; SRC-003 (interior) is an unpublished 2022 preprint; gluing theorem not peer-reviewed.; Whether the exterior data arise from an open set of AF initial data is not claimed.

**Falsifiers.**

## T-528 — Hintz 2026: nonlinear stability of subextremal Kerr black holes in the full subextremal range [verified]
