Once the genericity notion for this class is named (genericity_kind and genericity_topology are
unresolved here and owned by L0/L1; H4 requires them to be named before any claim is filed), there
is a set G of data, generic in that named sense and bound before the data, such that for every data
set in G the MGHD admits a conformal completion with a complete future null infinity I+, and no
future-inextendible causal geodesic of finite affine length is visible from I+. Visibility uses the
single-q TAIL predicate: gamma is visible from I+ iff there exist q in I+ and t0 in [0,T) such that
the tail gamma([t0,T)) is contained in J^-(q) intersect M. This is a definition, not an asserted
equivalence with any other formulation (review HF-06). The set-based reading (gamma contained in the
union of J^-(q) over all q in I+) is registered as variant `SET` (parent class AF-WCC-VAC-GEN) and is
not a predicate of this class; the two readings must never be interchanged.
