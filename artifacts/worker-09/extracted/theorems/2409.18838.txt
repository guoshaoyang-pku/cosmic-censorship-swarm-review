[theorem 2] Remark 3.20 (Relation to global inextendibility result). The above theorem serves as one piece in a global low-regularity inextendibility statement of generic rotating vacuum black hole spacetimes. Such statements are proven by contradiction. Assuming the existence of a Cloc0,1C^{0,1}_{\mathrm{loc}}-extension, one knows that there is an inextendible timelike geodesic in the original spacetime that has a limit point in the extension (Theorem 2 in [9], Theorem 3.2 in [29], see also [23]). One then leads this statement to a contradiction by showing that none of the inextendible timelike geodesics can leave into a Cloc0,1C^{0,1}_{\mathrm{loc}}-extension. Here, one has to take the different geometries along those geodesics into account. Theorem 3.14 deals with those timelike geodesics approaching the weak null singularity, e.g. τ2\tau_{2} in Figure 1. For timelike geodesics lying outside the black hole, for example τ1\tau_{1} in Figure 1, one uses their completeness to derive a contradiction [9]. The geometry in the interior of the black hole to the future of the weak null singularity is still not well-understood, but see for example [1], [37], [38] for scenarios involving and evidence for additional spacelike singularities.
